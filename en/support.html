Support

Taksi Kariyer 2 · Questions and help

Contact For questions, suggestions or bug reports, write to support@odexi.app. If you can, include your iPhone model, iOS version and when the problem started. Emails are usually answered within two business days.

A pack I bought is missing Open the Store panel in the game and tap Restore my purchases. You need to be signed in with the Apple account you used for the purchase. Packs also unlock on their own once you are back online.

I changed phones. Where is my progress? If you are signed in to iCloud with the same Apple account on the new iPhone, your progress comes back from iCloud. If the two devices have different saves, the game asks which one to continue with.

How do I change my ad preference? In the game, go to Settings > Ad preference. You can also turn off iOS tracking in your iPhone's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Refunds Purchases are made through Apple; to request a refund, use https://reportaproblem.apple.com.

Language The game is available in English and Turkish. It follows your iPhone's language, and you can change it in Settings.
