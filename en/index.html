Privacy Policy

Taksi Kariyer 2 · Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains which personal data is processed when you use Taksi Kariyer 2, for what purpose, and what rights you have. It also serves as the information notice required by Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

Summary

The game does not require an account. It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, camera or microphone. Your game progress and settings are stored on your device and are not sent to us; if you are signed in to iCloud on your iPhone, your progress is also written to the iCloud space reserved for this app in your Apple account. The external services that process data are Google AdMob for rewarded ads, and RevenueCat and the App Store for purchases. Personalized ads are shown only if you allow them.

Data controller

Data controller: Ali Rıza Yüksektepe. You can send requests and questions to support@odexi.app.

Data stored on your device

Money, vehicles, career progress, fleet, in-game time, settings (sound, graphics, steering preference) and the tips you have seen are stored in the app's storage on your device. This data is not personal data and is not sent to us.

If your device backup is turned on, this data may be included in the backup. Backups are provided by Apple through your account; we have no access to them.

Game progress written to iCloud (iPhone and iPad only)

If you are signed in to iCloud on your iPhone or iPad, your game progress is written to Apple's iCloud key-value store (NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore). This space belongs to your Apple account and is reserved for this app only. It contains money, reputation, license, vehicle and part condition, career, fleet, in-game time, tips shown, daily ad reward counters, trip count and a random game ID created by the game. Device-specific settings such as sound, graphics and steering are not written.

This data is stored on Apple's servers under your account. We cannot access it, it does not reach our servers and it is not shared with third parties. Its purpose is to bring back your progress when you reinstall the app or move to a new device with the same Apple account. If you are not signed in to iCloud, the game does not write to this space and your progress stays on your device only.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game has only rewarded ads that you start yourself; there are no interstitial ads. When you choose to watch a rewarded ad, it is served by Google AdMob. While loading and showing the ad, Google processes your IP address, device model, operating system and app version, your interaction with the ad, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. If you give permission, your advertising identifier (IDFA) is also used to pick ads that suit you. If you do not, non-personalized ads are shown; these ads also use the technical data listed above for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. You can read how Google processes this data here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your ad choice

Before showing an ad, the game asks whether you allow personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad preference. In the European Union, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, this question is asked through Google's consent form, and Google's privacy options are available from the same Settings section. If you allow personalization, iOS also asks whether the app may track you; you can turn this off in your iPhone's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Not giving permission does not prevent you from using the game.

Purchases (RevenueCat, App Store)

Payments are collected by Apple; your card details never reach us. To verify, deliver and restore purchases, a randomly generated user ID, transaction ID, product, price, currency and country are sent to RevenueCat. This ID does not reveal your name or Apple account to us. The list of packs you bought is kept on your device so that they stay unlocked without an internet connection.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect advertising IDs, device IDs, IP addresses, location or usage statistics; the data listed above for ads and purchases goes directly to Google and RevenueCat. The analytics, crash reporting and diagnostics services of the Unity engine, which the game is built with, are turned off.

Purposes and legal bases

Game data stored on your device is used only to run the game and keep your progress. The copy in iCloud is accessible only from your Apple account. Verifying, delivering and restoring purchases is based on performance of a contract (KVKK Art. 5/2-c). Showing non-personalized ads and preventing ad fraud is based on legitimate interest (Art. 5/2-f). Personalized ads and the use of the advertising identifier are based on your explicit consent (Art. 5/1). If you contact us by email, your email address and the content of your message are processed to answer your request under Art. 5/2-ç (legal obligation) and Art. 5/2-f (legitimate interest).

Transfers abroad

Data is transferred to Google LLC (AdMob) for ads, to RevenueCat, Inc. for purchase verification and to Apple for payment. These companies' servers are located outside Türkiye, mainly in the United States. Transfers abroad are made through the methods set out in KVKK Art. 9: appropriate safeguards offered by the service provider, or the occasional transfer cases under Art. 9/6 (when the transfer is necessary for the performance of a contract, or with your explicit consent). Game progress written to iCloud is synced and stored by Apple through your account under Apple's own terms. If you contact us by email, your message reaches us through the email service we use.

Retention

Game data on your device is kept until you delete the app. RevenueCat and the App Store keep purchase records for the periods required by tax and consumer law. Google's retention of ad data is subject to Google's policy. Game progress written to iCloud stays in your Apple account until you delete it; deleting the app does not delete it. Requests you send us are kept for the legal periods after they are answered.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes the game data on your device. You can delete the copy in your device backup from your iCloud account. You can reset your advertising identifier in your iPhone's settings. You can email us to request deletion of your purchase record at RevenueCat; the purchase date and product are enough for us to find it. Once the purchase record is deleted, purchases cannot be restored.

To delete game progress written to iCloud, open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your Apple Account name, go to iCloud, manage account storage, select Taksi Kariyer 2 and delete its data. After that, the game finds no record in iCloud and continues from the record on your device only. If you do not want anything written to iCloud, you can turn off iCloud access for Taksi Kariyer 2 in the same iCloud section.

Children

The game is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from this age group. Users under 18 must get the consent of a parent or guardian before making a purchase.

Security

Game data is not sent to a server of ours and we keep no database. Ad and purchase data is processed by Google and RevenueCat under their own security measures.

Your rights under KVKK

Under KVKK Art. 11 you have the right to:

  • Learn whether your personal data is processed and, if so, request information about it.
  • Learn the purpose of processing and whether the data is used in line with that purpose.
  • Know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom the data is transferred.
  • Ask for incomplete or incorrect data to be corrected.
  • Ask for the data to be deleted or destroyed within the framework of KVKK Art. 7.
  • Ask that correction, deletion or destruction be notified to the third parties the data was transferred to.
  • Object to a result against you that arises from analysis of the data exclusively by automated systems.
  • Claim compensation if you suffer damage because of unlawful processing.

You can send your requests to support@odexi.app. Requests are answered free of charge within thirty days at the latest. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you also have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection under the GDPR, and you can lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Changes

This policy is updated when a new service is added to the game. The current version is published on this page and the last update date is shown at the top.
